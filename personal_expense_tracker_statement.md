# Project Statement: Personal Expense Tracker

## 1. Problem Statement
Managing personal finances effectively is a common challenge for many individuals. Without a dedicated system, tracking daily expenditures, recurring subscriptions, and overall income often leads to:
* **Unclear Financial Visibility:** Difficulty understanding where money is being spent each month.
* **Overspending & Lack of Budget Control:** Lack of real-time alerts or tracking mechanisms to prevent exceeding planned budget limits.
* **Manual & Inefficient Record-Keeping:** Relying on physical receipts or messy spreadsheets, which are prone to human error and difficult to maintain over time.
* **Poor Financial Planning:** Absence of visual reports and insights to help users identify spending patterns and make informed saving decisions.

The **Personal Expense Tracker** addresses these challenges by providing a centralized, user-friendly digital system to monitor income, manage expenditures, and maintain financial discipline.

---

## 2. Scope of the Project

### Included in Scope:
* **Transaction Management:** Ability to record, edit, filter, and delete income and expense transactions.
* **Categorization System:** Pre-defined and customizable categories (e.g., Food, Rent, Entertainment, Utilities, Salary).
* **Budgeting & Spending Limits:** Setting monthly budgets per category and receiving threshold alerts when approaching or exceeding limits.
* **Analytics & Visual Reporting:** Graphical charts (e.g., pie charts, bar graphs) to analyze monthly spending breakdowns and income vs. expense ratios.
* **Data Persistence & Management:** Secure storage of user transactions using a local database or cloud backend, with options to export financial summaries (CSV/PDF).

### Out of Scope:
* Direct integration with real-world banking APIs for automatic live transaction sync.
* In-app money transfers or payment gateway processing.
* Investment management, stock tracking, or real-time currency conversion.

---

## 3. Target Users
* **Students & Young Professionals:** Individuals looking for a simple tool to manage tight monthly budgets and avoid unnecessary expenditures.
* **Freelancers & Gig Workers:** Users needing to keep track of variable income sources and business-related personal expenses.
* **Households & Families:** Anyone seeking clarity on recurring household utility expenses and category-wise spending habits.
* **Beginner Savers:** People aiming to build better financial habits through visual feedback and budget limits.

---

## 4. High-Level Features
1. **User Authentication & Profiles:** Secure user sign-up, login, and personalized data management.
2. **Interactive Financial Dashboard:** Quick overview of total balance, total income, total expenses, and recent activities.
3. **Expense & Income Logging:** Simple forms to quickly add transactions with date, amount, category, and notes.
4. **Category & Budget Management:** Tools to create custom categories and assign maximum budget caps.
5. **Data Visualization & Insights:** Graphical displays (charts/graphs) detailing expense distribution over weekly, monthly, or yearly periods.
6. **Search & Filter Operations:** Filter transactions by date range, category, payment method, or transaction type.
7. **Export Capabilities:** Option to download expense reports in CSV or PDF format for offline records.
